1. Service

Cat Utopia Rush is a portrait, cat-themed layered puzzle game offered through TikTok Mini Games. Players select only uncovered cat cards and place them in a seven-slot tray. Three cards of the same cat type in the tray are cleared. Removing cards reveals cards in lower layers. Clear both the board and the tray to win. If the tray remains full after matching cards have been cleared, the round ends in a loss. Players can restart the puzzle or play again and can use optional hints.
